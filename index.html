What it is

One link that holds everything about you

A paper card runs out, gets lost, and goes stale the moment you change your number. A digital card is just a web page — yours — with your phone, email, website and socials on it.

You share it as a link or let people scan your QR. It opens straight in their browser. Nobody installs an app, not you and not them.

Questions

Things people usually ask

Do I need to create an account?

No. Fill the form, tap Generate, and your card is live. You get an edit link at the end — save it, because that is how you change your card later.

Does the other person need an app?

No. Your card is a normal web page. They scan the QR or open the link and it works in whatever browser they already have.

Can I change my details after sharing the card?

Yes, using the edit link you got when you created the card. The link and QR stay the same, so everyone who already has it sees the updated details.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no trial, and we do not ask for card details.

What happens to my information?

Whatever you put on your card is public — that is the point of a business card. We do not sell it and we do not send you marketing.

Can I print the QR code?

Yes. Download it from the page after you generate your card and put it on a poster, a shop counter, or the back of a paper card.

What if I lose my edit link?

The card keeps working, but you will not be able to change it. Send the edit link to yourself on WhatsApp when you create the card — the page offers to do this for you.
